I am a doctoral researcher at the [Department of Computer Science, Aalto University](https://www.aalto.fi/en/department-of-computer-science), where I'm fortunate to be advised by [Prof. Eric Malmi](https://ericmalmi.com/) and [Prof. Pekka Marttinen](https://users.ics.aalto.fi/~pemartti/). My work is supported by the Finland Fellowship and the [Finnish Center for Artificial Intelligence (FCAI)](https://fcai.fi/ai-doc/about).

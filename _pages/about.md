I am a doctoral researcher at the [Department of Computer Science, Aalto University](https://www.aalto.fi/en/department-of-computer-science), where I'm fortunate to be advised by [Prof. Pekka Marttinen](https://users.ics.aalto.fi/~pemartti/) and [Prof. Eric Malmi](https://ericmalmi.com/). My work is supported by the Finland Fellowship and the [Finnish Center for Artificial Intelligence (FCAI)](https://fcai.fi/ai-doc/about). My research interests are in large language models, particularly adaptive computation and mixture-of-experts models.

Prior to my PhD, I worked as a Software Engineering Intern at [QuantumBlack AI Labs](https://www.mckinsey.com/capabilities/quantumblack/labs), McKinsey's AI-focused division. Before that, I completed my MSc in Artificial Intelligence at [Queen Mary University of London](http://www.eecs.qmul.ac.uk/) as a recipient of the [Google DeepMind Scholarship](https://deepmind.google/about/education/#scholarships) and the [QuantumBlack (McKinsey) & Office of AI Scholarship](https://www.qmul.ac.uk/eecs/news-and-events/news/items/scholarships-with-quantumblack-a-mckinsey-company-are-available-for-postgraduate-students-at-queen-mary-applications-are-open.html). I received my B.Eng. in Computer Engineering from [Ahmadu Bello University](https://abu.edu.ng/).

I am always interested in learning, collaborating, and discussing new research ideas. :)
